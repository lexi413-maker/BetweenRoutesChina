# content_calendar.md

## Posting rhythm
Frequency: 4 posts per week — optimal for algorithmic growth on new accounts.
Format split: 2 Reels + 2 static images per week.
Best posting times (CET / target audience timezone): Tuesday & Thursday 7–9am, Wednesday & Friday 12–1pm. Adjust once account analytics are available.

## Weekly content structure
Monday — Reel — B2B insight: process, behind-the-scenes, or "how we work"
Wednesday — Static — China context: place, culture, or industry observation
Friday — Reel — Story-led: a real situation, problem solved, or client scenario
Sunday — Static — Ambient / visual: texture, mood, place. Lighter tone.

## Content pillars
1. How we work (40%) — Process transparency. What does a commercial visit actually look like? What do we handle? This builds trust with B2B prospects.
2. China unlocked (30%) — Context a foreign visitor wouldn't easily find. Not tourist facts — industry insight, regional nuance, practical knowledge.
3. Stories & scenarios (20%) — Real or illustrative situations. "A client needed X. Here's how it went." Narrative-driven, specific, human.
4. Ambient China (10%) — Beautiful, warm, considered visuals. No caption needed beyond a single line. Grid filler that maintains visual cohesion.

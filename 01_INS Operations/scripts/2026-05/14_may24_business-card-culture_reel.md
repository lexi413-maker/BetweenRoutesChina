The business card is not just a card.

In a Chinese supplier meeting, how you give and receive a card signals who you are before you say a word.

🃏 Give it with both hands. A one-handed handover reads as casual — fine in Europe, slightly dismissive in a formal Chinese context.

👀 Receive it, read it, place it on the table. Don't pocket it immediately. That's the visual equivalent of not listening.

📋 If you're at a table, arrange received cards in the same order as the people sitting opposite you. It shows you know who you're talking to.

🈳 If you have cards printed in Chinese on the reverse — even basic title/company — it's noticed. Positively.

These aren't rigid rules. They're signals. And in a room where you're asking for trust, every signal counts.

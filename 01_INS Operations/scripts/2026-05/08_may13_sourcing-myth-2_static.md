Sourcing myth #2: "My agent handles everything remotely."

Your agent sends emails. That's not the same as being in the room.

On-site, you learn things that never make it into a message:
→ The hesitation before a price is confirmed
→ The production floor that doesn't match the capacity claims
→ The owner who pulls you aside at lunch to offer a different deal

Remote relationships work. Until they don't.

The businesses that visit — even once — renegotiate better, catch problems earlier, and build supplier loyalty faster.

It's not about distrust. It's about information that only exists in person.

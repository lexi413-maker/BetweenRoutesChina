A client came to visit Supplier A.

Supplier A was fine. Nothing wrong. But on the way through the production floor, we passed a secondary finishing line that wasn't on the visit agenda.

We asked about it.

Turned out: the factory had invested in a new lamination process six months earlier. Hadn't updated their Alibaba page. Our client was sourcing that exact component from a third party in Germany.

Same quality. One-third of the cost. Three weeks faster on lead time.

The supplier didn't think to mention it.
We knew to ask.

The visit was booked for Supplier A. The deal was signed with Supplier A — plus a new lamination contract.

What to send your interpreter before you land in China.

Most people don't send anything. That's the first mistake.

Send us:
→ Your product spec sheet or category overview
→ The key questions you need answered at each supplier
→ Any red lines (certifications, MOQ, lead time limits)
→ Names and titles of who you'll meet

We translate this into a briefing document — in both languages — and share it with the interpreter before day one.

The meeting is more focused. The interpreter asks the right follow-up questions. You leave with real answers.

Preparation is the service.

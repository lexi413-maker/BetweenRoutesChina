A client came to us with 4 supplier appointments. 2 days. Shenzhen and Dongguan.

Here's how we made it work.

Day 1: Shenzhen
→ Factory A — 9:30am. Finished by 11:45. Supplier 2 pushed to 2pm because of production floor timing.
→ Factory B — 2:15pm. We restructured the briefing agenda on the way over.

Day 2: Dongguan
→ 70-minute drive. We used it to prep the team for factory C's management style.
→ Factory C confirmed. Factory D? The owner cancelled at 8am.
→ We had a backup lined up by 9am.

4 visits. 2 days. 1 signed LOI.

The backup mattered more than the original plan.

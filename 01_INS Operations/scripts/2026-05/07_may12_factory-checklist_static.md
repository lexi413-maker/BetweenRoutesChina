5 things to confirm before booking your factory tour.

1. Is the factory currently in active production — or showing you a showroom?
2. Do they export to your market? (EU compliance isn't assumed.)
3. Who will be in the meeting — owner, sales, or floor manager?
4. Can you access the production area, or is it restricted?
5. What's the realistic MOQ for your category?

These questions save you a 4-hour trip to the wrong room.

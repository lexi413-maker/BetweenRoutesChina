3 signs your Chinese supplier is actually trustworthy 🧵

1. They say no to some of your requests.
Suppliers who agree to everything are hiding something. Good ones know their limits.

2. They invite you to see the production floor unannounced.
Confidence = transparency. Nervousness about a surprise visit? Ask yourself why.

3. They ask about your end customer.
A supplier thinking about your client's needs is thinking long-term — not just filling an order.

Planning a China trip in May? Read this first.

May 1-5 is Golden Week (劳动节) — China's Labor Day holiday.

What this means for buyers:
→ Most factories close for 3-5 days minimum
→ Logistics & customs run on skeleton staff
→ Supplier response times slow the week before AND after

If you're targeting a May visit, book for May 6+ and build buffer into your schedule.
